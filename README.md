# AoE2 Counter Lookup

A quick in-game reference for Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition. Look up any unit to see what beats it, what it beats, and how its damage works. Or pick the civ you're facing to see its whole army.

## Features

- **Unit search.** Type a unit or a nickname (`arb`, `halb`, `pally`, `onager`) to see:
  - **Beat it with:** what to build against it
  - **Don't send:** what it beats
  - **Damage info:** melee or pierce damage, bonus damage, armor classes, and which units take bonus damage against it
- **Civilization view.** Pick a civ to see its unique units plus every regular unit it can build. Missing units and missing final upgrades (for example "No Paladin") are called out.
- **Clickable counters.** Click any unit in a card to jump to it.
- **Filters** by unit type, plus keyboard shortcuts: `/` to search, `Esc` to clear.
- Works on desktop and phone, in light and dark mode.

## Using it

Open the GitHub Pages link, or download `index.html` and double-click it. It's one self-contained file, so it also works offline.

## Notes on the data

- Civ unit rosters come from the [aoe2techtree.net](https://aoe2techtree.net) data file.
- The counters and damage info are general Definitive Edition knowledge for learning matchups. They are not exact stats. Upgrades, civ bonuses and patches all change the details.
- Ship upgrades aren't tracked.
