How damage works
  • Melee damage is reduced by the target's melee armor. Pierce damage (arrows, bullets, bolts) is reduced by pierce armor.
  • Bonus damage is extra damage against an armor class. Spearmen hit anything in the Cavalry class extra hard; skirmishers do the same to the Archer class.
  • A unit can be in several classes. A cavalry archer is in Archer and Cavalry archer, so skirmishers' bonus hits it.
  • Most counters come from this. Check "Takes bonus from" on any card to see which regular units are built to beat it.
